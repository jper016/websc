Payment Instructions:

  1. Booking an Online Appointment
    • Upon booking, a Payment Reference will be generated.
    • Your Payment Reference will be displayed on the confirmation page after booking, and you may download and print a copy.
    • A copy of the Payment Reference will also be sent to your email.
    Note: A reference number can only be used once.
  2. Payment of Fees
    • Pay the exact amount. Any excess payment will be forfeited. Likewise, payment with less than the total amount indicated will not be processed.
    • The total amount due is inclusive of convenience fee.
    Note: If paying for multiple reference numbers, kindly pay separately for each reference number. A unique reference number is generated per transaction.
  3. Receiving your confirmation email
    • A confirmation email will be sent once payment has been successfully processed.
    • This email will serve as proof that your appointment is confirmed.
  4. Additional information
    • If you made a short payment by mistake, do not attempt to correct it by making another payment transaction with the same reference number. Please contact our Helpdesk immediately at (02) 8234 3488.
